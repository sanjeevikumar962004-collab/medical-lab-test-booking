Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a home sample collection in Salem?

You can book directly by calling +91 98765 43210, sending us a message on WhatsApp, or filling out the contact form above. Our phlebotomist will arrive at your home at the requested time slot.

How quickly will I receive my test reports?

Most routine blood tests (CBC, Lipid Profile, Blood Sugar, Thyroid) are reported within 6 hours. You will receive an SMS and WhatsApp alert with a secure download link for your PDF report.

Do I need to fast before my blood test?

Fasting (8-12 hours) is required for tests like Fasting Blood Sugar, Lipid Profile, and Full Body Packages. Our team will guide you on exact instructions when confirming your appointment.
